# Artifact Type: Unknown / Custom

## Conservative handling

Unknown artifacts default to preservation, not deletion.

Determine:

- who reads/executes it;
- whether its path/name is significant;
- whether it is runtime, distribution, validation, development, or documentation material;
- explicit inbound/outbound references;
- whether modification is actually required.

If the artifact may be active and its contract cannot be established, preserve it unchanged and block destructive deletion of the only source copy.

Do not force an unknown surface into a known artifact kind merely to simplify the plan.
