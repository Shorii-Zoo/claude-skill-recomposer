# Artifact Type: Rule / Persistent Policy Surface

## Contract

Determine scope and loading semantics before transformation:

- always loaded vs conditional;
- repository/project/user/global scope;
- host-native rule mechanism vs ordinary reference prose;
- precedence relative to skill policy and user instruction.

## Recomposition

A broad rule MAY become a topic invariant or shared policy only if the new scope preserves activation and authority.

A specialized rule SHOULD move down to the narrowest valid topic/skill rather than remain broadly loaded.

## Do not

Do not convert broad always-active policy into passive knowledge or widen a local rule simply because several skills mention it.
