# Artifact Type: Reference / Template / Asset

## Role

References are passive support unless another artifact executes or interprets them specially.

Classify whether the material is:

- knowledge/reference;
- template;
- example;
- schema/specification;
- static asset;
- generated output seed.

## Recomposition

Deduplicate semantically equivalent references and place shared material at the narrowest scope covering all consumers.

Rewrite links after moves. Preserve literal templates/assets when their exact form matters.

Binary assets are preserved as artifacts; they are not semantically rewritten.
